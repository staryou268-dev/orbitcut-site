Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

OrbitCut is a Mac app that shows a radial menu of keyboard shortcuts. It was built to work without collecting information about you. This page explains exactly what the app and this website do with data. In this policy, “we” means Saran Teja, who makes and sells OrbitCut.

The short version

  • OrbitCut has no accounts and no analytics, and it does not show ads.
  • Your menus, settings and usage counts stay on your Mac.
  • The app contacts the internet only to check your licence key and to look for updates.
  • We never read what you type or what is inside your documents.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your menus and settings, including any custom icon images you add.
  • Usage counts. If “Learn from my shortcut usage” is on, OrbitCut counts how many times you use each Command shortcut in each app, for example “Command R in Safari, 12 times”. It uses these counts to suggest slices. It does not record what you type, only which shortcut was used. You can turn this off or erase the counts in Settings.
  • Your trial start date and licence key, which are stored in the macOS Keychain.
  • A daily counter for the free plan.

Why OrbitCut needs Accessibility access

macOS requires this permission for any app that presses shortcuts on your behalf. OrbitCut uses it to send the shortcut you choose to the app you are using, to notice when you press your OrbitCut shortcut, and to read the names and shortcuts of menu items in other apps so it can build menus for them. It does not read the contents of your documents or windows, and none of this information is sent anywhere.

What the app sends over the internet

  • Licence activation and checks. When you activate a key, and about once a week afterwards, the app sends your licence key, the name of your Mac and a unique activation ID to Lemon Squeezy, the service that issues and verifies our licences. If you are offline, the app keeps working.
  • Update checks. About once a day the app asks this website for a small file that lists the latest version number. Our web host can see the request, including your IP address, as with any web request.

The app sends nothing else.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record. They collect your payment details, name, email address and country, and they handle sales tax. We never see your card number. We receive your name, email address, country and order details so we can send your licence and give you support. Lemon Squeezy's own privacy policy applies to the data they hold.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it loads no fonts or scripts from other sites. If that ever changes, we will update this page first.

How long we keep information

We keep order and support records for as long as needed to provide support, handle refunds and meet our legal and tax obligations. Because the app stores its own data on your Mac, deleting the app and its settings removes that data.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to records we must keep by law. Email staryou268@gmail.com.

Children

OrbitCut is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes we will say so on the home page.
