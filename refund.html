Refund policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

We want you to be happy with OrbitCut. If it is not right for you, you can have your money back within 14 days of buying.

Try it first

Every download includes a free 7-day trial with all features, and a free plan after that. We encourage you to use the trial before you buy, so you know it works well on your Mac and with your apps.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email staryou268@gmail.com within 14 days of your purchase.
  2. Include the email address you used to buy, or your order number from the receipt.
  3. You do not need to explain why, although we would love to know what went wrong so we can improve OrbitCut.

What happens next

  • We confirm your request and start the refund, usually within a couple of business days.
  • The refund goes back to the payment method you used. Your bank or card provider decides how long it takes to appear, and this can take several days.
  • Your licence key is switched off when the refund is issued. The free plan continues to work on your Mac.

Taxes and fees

The refund covers the full amount you paid, including any sales tax or VAT that was charged at checkout.

Problems we can fix

If something is not working, please write to us first. Many problems can be solved quickly, and we would like the chance to help. A refund is always available within the 14 days, whether or not we can fix the problem.
